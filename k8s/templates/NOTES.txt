Mesada Supabase backend is starting.

Public URL (via Traefik + Kong):
  https://{{ .Values.ingress.host }}

Point the app at it:
  EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL=https://{{ .Values.ingress.host }}
  EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=<same anon-key passed via --set auth.anonKey>

Secrets live in Secret/{{ include "mesada.fullname" . }}-secrets
(postgres-password, jwt-secret, anon-key, service-role-key).
Generate values with: node infra/gen-keys.mjs
